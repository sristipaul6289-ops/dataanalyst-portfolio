E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

Microsoft Excel | Power Query | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Excel Formulas

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, customers, products, shipping methods, discounts, and regional performance.

  • Cleaned and prepared data using Power Query.
  • Built KPI metrics including Total Sales, Total Profit, Orders, Quantity, Average Order Value, and Profit Margin.
  • Performed customer segment, product, regional, shipping, and discount analysis.
  • Created PivotTables, PivotCharts, and an interactive dashboard using slicers.
  • Analyzed top-performing products, categories, and sub-categories.

Key Insight: Technology generated the highest sales among the three product categories in the analyzed dataset, while discount levels showed a noticeable impact on profitability.
