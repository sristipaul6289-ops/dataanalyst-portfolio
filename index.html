Microsoft Excel | Power Query | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Excel Formulas | Slicers

  • Cleaned and transformed raw e-commerce data using Power Query.
  • Developed six KPIs: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Average Order Value and Profit Margin.
  • Performed sales and profitability analysis across categories, sub-categories and products.

Key Insight: Technology generated the highest sales among the three product categories, while higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profitability.
