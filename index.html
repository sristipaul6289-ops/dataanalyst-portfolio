E-Commerce Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

Microsoft Excel | Power Query | PivotTables | PivotCharts | Excel Formulas | Slicers

Built an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze e-commerce sales, profitability, customer segments, products, discounts, shipping methods, and regional performance, transforming raw data into actionable business insights.

  • Cleaned and transformed raw e-commerce data using Power Query and prepared the dataset for analysis.
  • Developed six business KPIs: Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Total Quantity, Average Order Value, and Profit Margin.
  • Performed sales and profitability analysis across product categories, sub-categories, top products, customer segments, discounts, shipping modes, and regions.
  • Built PivotTables and PivotCharts to analyze key business metrics and identify performance trends.
  • Created an interactive Excel dashboard with slicers for Region, Customer Segment, and Ship Mode.
  • Identified Technology as the highest-sales product category with approximately $5.98M in sales.

Key Insight: Technology generated the highest sales among the three product categories, while the analysis showed that higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profitability.
